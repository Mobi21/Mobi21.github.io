Technology that meets people where they are.

Seneca Signal · First place, PowerUpTech 2025

Neighborhood information should be reachable even when Wi-Fi isn’t.

Our team designed Seneca Signal for Buffalo’s Seneca-Babcock neighborhood: an admin dashboard, mobile-friendly website, and SMS interface connecting community information.

I led UI/UX and architecture, helped plan the product, and presented the winning demo. We later demonstrated the solution at the Microsoft-sponsored TechConnect 2025 AI for Social Good conference.

The most interesting constraint was accessibility in the everyday sense: making useful information reachable through the devices and connections people already had.
