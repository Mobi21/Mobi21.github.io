About Me

I'm a Computer Science and Psychology dual-major at UMBC, where I'm a Meyerhoff Scholar. I'm drawn to the intersection of software engineering and human behavior — building systems that are technically rigorous and thoughtful about the people using them.

Lately I've been focused on local-first AI agents, developer tooling, and the evaluation layer around LLMs. My recent work at M&T Bank, JuneBrain, and in UMBC research has ranged from NLP pipelines to computer vision to web-scraping and policy analysis.
