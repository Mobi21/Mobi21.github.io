    A connected business workspace.

    Client information should travel with the work.

    KS App brings CRM, project delivery, finance, knowledge, and client portals into one workspace. Its AI operator uses the same domain services as the human interface, so a request is grounded in the application’s records and permissions.

    The engineering underneath.

    Permissions belong to the application.

    Workspace identity and allowed operations come from server-owned context. The model does not get to choose its tenant, role, or credentials.

    Skills guide. Tools act.

    Versioned skill guidance and on-demand tool discovery expose the operations needed for the work. Tools call shared business services instead of inventing a second data layer.

    Keep the record of what happened.

    Persistent conversations, durable workflows, approvals, and action receipts support recovery after interruptions. Uncertain external actions need reconciliation before another attempt.
